From parents in the beta

Common questions

Will it delete my photos?

No. Your photos stay exactly where they are. Choosing which moments to keep never deletes anything from your phone.

What if I have tens of thousands of photos?

That’s who it’s for. You start seeing moments right away while the rest get ready.

Do my photos leave my phone?

Your photos stay on your phone and in your own iCloud. To write each moment’s title and note, a few small previews, with location and camera data removed, are sent to an AI model. We don’t run servers that keep your photos.

Do I have to organize first?

No setup and no albums to make. Open it and start.

What does it cost?

We’ll decide pricing after the beta. Everyone who joins the beta gets an exclusive discount offer.
