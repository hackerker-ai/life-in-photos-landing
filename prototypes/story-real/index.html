Common questions

Will it delete my photos?

No. Keep, Later and Skip only change what's in this app. Nothing in your Photos library is deleted or moved.

How long does it take?

About five minutes. Each session is ten moments, and you can stop partway and come back.

What happens to a moment I skip?

It leaves your list in the app. The photos themselves stay exactly where they were in Photos. If you can't decide, Later holds the moment for you.

Which iPhones does it work on?

iPhones running iOS 26 or later. The beta is installed through TestFlight.
